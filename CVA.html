Project Evidence

What the Project Owner Shared

Organization's Needs Her Bold Tribe needed an organized and dependable data collection and management process for its Southeast Creatives Bootcamp 2026. With participants, volunteers, facilitators, and officials involved in a multi-day programme, the organization required a team to manage essential information and maintain accurate programme records. The organization needed to:
- Establish an organized system for collecting and managing participants' registration information.
- Monitor daily attendance and maintain records of participants, volunteers, and officials.
- Ensure the accuracy, completeness, and proper organization of collected data.
- Document daily programme activities and maintain records for reporting purposes.
- Track the distribution of bootcamp materials and maintain accountability for available resources.
- Identify missing information, inconsistencies, and administrative gaps.
- Provide proper documentation to support programme monitoring, evaluation, and future planning.

The overall expectation was to ensure that important information generated throughout the bootcamp was accurately captured, properly managed, and documented.
